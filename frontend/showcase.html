What is AutoResto?+

AutoResto is a restaurant technology platform that connects QR table ordering, digital menus, restaurant order workflows, billing and operational tools.

Do guests need to install an app?+

No. Guests can scan a restaurant table QR code and use the digital menu in their mobile browser.

How can I manage my digital menu?+

Restaurant owners can use the existing owner workspace to manage menu items, categories, prices and availability.

What payment options are available?+

The application supports cash and the configured Cashfree checkout flow. The options shown to guests depend on the restaurant setup.

Can I manage orders and kitchen workflows?+

Yes. Orders can be viewed in the restaurant workspaces, with kitchen and KOT functionality available where configured for the operation.

How do I register my restaurant?+

Choose “Become a Partner” or “Register” to open the existing AutoResto restaurant registration flow. Any verification or approval steps remain part of that process.
